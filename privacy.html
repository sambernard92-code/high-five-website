High Five Fantasy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

High Five Fantasy is a free five-a-side fantasy football game. This page explains what information the app keeps about you, why, who helps us, and what you can do about it.

Who we are

High Five Fantasy is run by Sam Bernard, an individual based in the United Kingdom. I'm responsible for your information (the "controller"). For anything about your information, email hello@highfivefantasy.com.

What we keep

  • Your email address, used to sign you in with a one-time code.
  • Your profile: your username, avatar colour, the rewards you've unlocked and what you've chosen to show (such as your pitch, name tags, league row style and badge).
  • A record that you've confirmed you're 18 or over, which you do when you set up your profile.
  • Your game activity: the teams you pick and when you saved them, your scores, the leagues you're in, and your challenge progress.
  • Emojis you send and receive, and anyone you've muted.
  • Your settings, such as your notification choices. The app doesn't send notifications yet.
  • Technical records: when you sign in, our database provider records things like your IP address and device, to keep accounts secure and fix problems.

We don't ask for your real name, payment details or location. The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

You need to give an email address to sign in. Without it you can't play. Everything else comes from using the game.

Why we keep it, and our legal reasons

We don't make decisions about you by computer that have legal or similarly significant effects. Scores, league tables and teams for people who forget to pick are worked out automatically, but they only affect the game.

What other players can see

  • Other players in your leagues can see your username, avatar, badge, league row style, pitch style, scores and results.
  • Anyone signed in can see that you've saved a team. Nobody can see a player in your team until that player's match has kicked off.
  • When you send an emoji, only you and the person you send it to can see it. They see it with your username.
  • Nobody else can see your email address.

Who helps us run the app

We share only what each service needs, and we never sell your information.

  • Supabase stores the game's database, handles sign-in and keeps sign-in records. Our database is in London. Supabase's US company may access it to provide support.
  • Resend sends your sign-in emails, so it sees your email address. Resend is a US company.
  • Namecheap forwards emails sent to our contact address, and Google (Gmail) stores them, so they see anything you email us.
  • GitHub hosts this website, so it sees the IP address of anyone visiting these pages. It's a US company.
  • Apple, when you install the app through the App Store or TestFlight. Apple's own privacy policy covers this.

Football data comes from API-Football. None of your information is sent to it.

Where a service handles your information outside the UK, it's protected by the safeguards UK law requires, such as standard data protection clauses approved for UK transfers or the UK–US data bridge. Email us if you'd like more detail.

On your phone

The app stores your sign-in on your phone so you stay signed in, and a note of which goals it has already shown you. This is needed for the app to work and isn't used for tracking.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and game history: while you have an account.
  • Emojis: while you or the other person has an account. If either of you deletes their account, the emoji is deleted.
  • Your signed-in session, including your IP address and device: until you log out or delete your account.
  • Sign-in logs: usually a few days, and no longer than 90 days.
  • Sign-in emails: Resend keeps its email records for 30 days.
  • Emails you send us: for as long as we need them to deal with what you've asked.
  • Backups: any backups are deleted within 30 days.

When you delete your account, your email, profile, teams, rewards and emojis are deleted and you're removed from your leagues. Past league results may keep an anonymous ID with no name or email attached, so the tables still add up.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • get a copy of your information
  • have wrong information corrected
  • have your information deleted (you can also do this yourself in the app, under Profile, Settings, Delete account)
  • ask us to limit how we use your information
  • get your information in a format you can take elsewhere

Your right to object: where we use your information because of our legitimate interests (keeping the game safe and fair), you can object, and we'll stop unless we have a strong reason to carry on.

To use any of these rights, email hello@highfivefantasy.com. We'll reply within one month.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first at hello@highfivefantasy.com. We'll acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and get back to you as soon as we can. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Age

High Five Fantasy is only for people aged 18 or over. If you think someone under 18 has an account, please email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your information in an important way, we'll also try to tell you directly, such as by email.
